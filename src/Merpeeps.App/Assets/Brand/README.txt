Merpie locked brand assets

Approved 2026-10-08. The mascot combines the original flying magpie's head, eye
and feather streaks with the revised middle concept's broad split mermaid fin.
Source artwork was generated with user-approved Figma AI credits, then composed,
cleaned and vector-traced locally. The simplified icon is hand-authored SVG.
These are original project-owned brand assets, not Mermaid's logo.

merpie-mascot.svg/.png: transparent mascot for light surfaces.
merpie-mascot-dark.svg/.png: the same mascot with a thin light visibility keyline.
merpie-mark.svg/.png: transparent simplified icon mark.
merpie-app-icon.svg and sized PNGs: icon with an intentional fixed pale backplate.
merpie-app-icon.ico: Windows icon, 16/24/32/48/64/128/256px.
merpie-app-icon.icns: macOS icon, derived from the approved 1024px PNG.

Do not stretch, crop away the head/raised wing/split fin, substitute the earlier
wave-only tail, or recolor the semantic artwork when the surrounding app changes
theme. The fixed backplate is part of the app icon; outside it is transparent.
Choose the dark mascot variant explicitly for dark surfaces.

Product branding is Merpie. x-merpeeps, CLI/MCP names, namespaces, shared
library/package IDs and MERPEEPS_APP are retained for compatibility.
